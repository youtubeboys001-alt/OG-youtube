# THUMBNAIL DESIGN SYSTEM — "DEAD AIR" palette
Reusable art direction for channel thumbnails (cinematic, curiosity-gap, mobile-first).

## Core principles
1. ONE focal point, readable at tiny mobile size.
2. Desaturated cold base so 1–2 accent colors do all the shouting.
3. Text = white SETUP line + yellow PUNCHLINE line, bold condensed uppercase, top-left.
4. Leading lines (light, gas, gaze) point INTO the subject.
5. 3 depth layers: foreground element → mid subject+light → background silhouette.
6. Heavy vignette to lock the center.
7. Color should encode the video's thesis (e.g. comfort color vs threat color).

## "DEAD AIR" palette (Video 6 — Kalachi)
| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Base void | Nightfall | #0A0D12 |
| Night atmosphere | Steppe Dusk | #16202B |
| Buildings / mid-tone | Cold Concrete | #33414D |
| Warmth / false safety | Sodium Lie | #E7A33E |
| Threat / signature pop | Dead Air (toxic lime) | #A8E05F |
| Punchline text | Alarm | #FFD23F |
| Setup text / subject light | Bone | #F4F1E8 |

Tension: warm Sodium Lie (comfort) vs Dead Air green (poison) over a cold desaturated base.
The green is the ownable, brand-memorable accent.

## Text pattern
- Line 1 (white / Bone): the setup.
- Line 2 (yellow / Alarm): the reversal or gut-punch.
- Bold condensed uppercase, top-left, tight leading.

## Video 6 locked concept — "The Sleeper in the Street"
Lone flat 2D STICK-FIGURE villager asleep in an empty street (round head, dot eyes closed),
pinned in an amber streetlight pool; toxic-green gas curls up from the ground toward the body;
mine headframe bleeding green in the back. Cinematic lighting/palette, flat stick-figure subject.
Text: "A WHOLE VILLAGE FELL ASLEEP" / "AND THE AIR DID IT"
(alt: "IT WASN'T A DISEASE." / "IT WAS THE AIR.")

NOTE: subjects stay flat 2D stick-figure across ALL thumbnails (matches the videos); the
"cinematic" part is only the lighting, palette, depth and vignette — never realistic rendering.

## Palette-swap notes for future videos
Keep the SYSTEM (cold base + comfort-color vs threat-color + white/yellow text). Swap the two
accent hues to fit each video's thesis. Examples:
- Fear of the dark: cold base + warm safe-light vs deep void-black threat.
- Animal memory: cold base + warm human-light vs cold watching-eye cyan.
